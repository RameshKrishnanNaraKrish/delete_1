    Fix NoMethodError on truncated NUUO responses
    
    In Rex::Proto::Nuuo::Client#read_response, an EOFError, Errno::EPIPE,
    or IOError during body reading triggers the rescue block which attempts
    to set res.error = :truncated. Because Rex::Proto::Nuuo::Response lacked
    an :error accessor, this raised an unhandled NoMethodError instead of
    marking the response.
    
    Adds attr_accessor :error to Rex::Proto::Nuuo::Response and adds an
    RSpec unit test covering truncated response handling.
    
    Signed-off-by: Urval Kheni <kheniurval777@gmail.com>
